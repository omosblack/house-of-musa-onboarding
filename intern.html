Your role

Your track decides your assignments and your project work under Kore. Marketing interns also build toward the program’s AI presentation, presented live at a Wednesday meeting. All interns also work as a team on the House of Musa Fashion Week website design project below, whatever your track.

Headline project: design the House of Musa Fashion Week website

Alongside your track assignments, you will design the House of Musa Fashion Week website as a team. This is one of the main things you will be working on during the internship, and it is real work for the house, not a practice exercise.

  • What you are designing: the Fashion Week website for House of Musa, the online home for the show: the event, the collections and frames on the runway, and the story of the house.
  • How you will work: as an intern team under Kore, who sets the brief, splits the work across tracks, and reviews every stage. Research, page concepts, layout and visual design, copy, and imagery all run through Kore first.
  • Brand rules apply: the brand is House of Musa everywhere on the site, the real House of Musa logo only, eyewear front and center with the frames shown worn and close, and every picture of a person wearing a product links to the product they are wearing.
  • Tools: you will use the tools from your download step, Muse, Claude, WhatsApp, GitHub, and Zernio, all on your own free accounts, to plan, build, and share the work.
  • Approval: nothing goes live, and nothing is shared outside the team, without approval running through Kore and the founder first.

Your progress on the Fashion Week website is part of your check ins with Kore, your Wednesday meeting updates, and your Week 4, 8, and 12 gates.

ONE-WAY NON-DISCLOSURE AND OWNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Agreement is between Musa Group LLC, doing business as House of Musa (“House of Musa”), and the undersigned individual (“Recipient”), effective on the date Recipient signs below. It applies to employees, interns, contractors, creators, and suppliers engaged by House of Musa.

1. One-way agreement. House of Musa will share Confidential Information and work materials with Recipient so Recipient can do their work. This Agreement protects House of Musa only. It places obligations on Recipient and gives Recipient no rights in anything House of Musa shares or anything Recipient creates for House of Musa. House of Musa takes on no confidentiality or other obligation to Recipient under this Agreement.

2. What counts as Confidential Information. Everything Recipient sees, hears, receives, or creates through their work that is not public, including: unreleased designs, collections, tech packs, patterns, and samples; supplier, factory, and manufacturer identities, contacts, and pricing; wholesale and retail pricing strategy; customer, client, and partner lists and their contact details and order history; sales figures, revenue, budgets, and financial information; marketing plans, campaign concepts, launch dates, and ad account data; brand imagery, footage, and content before release; business plans and expansion plans; and personal information about the founder, team members, clients, and customers.

3. Recipient’s obligations. Recipient will: (a) keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and disclose it to no one outside House of Musa; (b) use it only to do their House of Musa work, and for no personal or outside purpose; (c) not copy, photograph, record, download, or remove Confidential Information except as their role requires; (d) not post, share, or hint at unreleased products, designs, campaigns, or business information on any personal or public channel, including social media, group chats, and portfolios, without written approval from House of Musa; (e) protect any device, account, or document holding Confidential Information with reasonable care, and report any loss or suspected exposure immediately.

4. Everything created here stays at House of Musa. Everything Recipient creates, contributes to, or produces in connection with House of Musa, including designs, garments, content, copy, photographs, video, edits, strategies, documents, data, contact lists, and account content, is a work made for hire and the sole and exclusive property of Musa Group LLC from the moment it is created. To the extent any right does not vest automatically, Recipient assigns all rights, title, and interest in it to Musa Group LLC, and will sign anything reasonably needed to confirm that ownership. Recipient may not take, keep, copy, remove, use, display (including in any portfolio), sell, license, or recreate any of it, during the engagement or after it ends, without written approval from House of Musa. Work created for House of Musa stays at House of Musa.

5. Accounts and access. Anything created or stored in House of Musa accounts, drives, and systems belongs to House of Musa. Recipient’s access is a licence to do their work only, and it ends when the engagement ends or whenever House of Musa revokes it.

6. No circumvention. Recipient will not use Confidential Information to contact House of Musa’s suppliers, manufacturers, clients, customers, or partners for their own benefit, for a competing business, or on behalf of anyone else, during their engagement and for twelve (12) months after it ends.

7. How long this lasts. The confidentiality obligations begin on the date Recipient signs, cover information received before signing if it was shared in expectation of engagement, and continue for three (3) years after Recipient’s engagement ends for any reason. Information that qualifies as a trade secret stays protected for as long as it remains secret. The ownership terms in sections 4 and 5 are permanent and survive the end of the engagement.

8. Return of materials. When the engagement ends, or whenever House of Musa asks, Recipient will promptly return or permanently delete all Confidential Information and House of Musa property in their possession, including copies on personal devices and accounts, and confirm the return or deletion in writing if asked.

9. If this Agreement is broken. Recipient understands that a breach may cause harm that money alone cannot repair, and that House of Musa may seek a court order stopping further disclosure or use, in addition to any other remedies available under law.

10. General. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the District of Columbia. It is the entire agreement between the parties about Confidential Information and ownership of work created for House of Musa, and replaces any earlier understanding on those subjects. If any part is held unenforceable, the rest still applies. Signing electronically, including typing Recipient’s full legal name and the date below, counts as Recipient’s signature.
